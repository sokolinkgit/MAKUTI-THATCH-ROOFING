HomeAbout

"We are Kenyan fundis with palm leaves in our blood. Give us timber and makuti — we'll give you shade, beauty, and a roof your grandchildren will admire."

Based in Nairobi and working countrywide, Makuti Thatch Roofing has woven 350+ roofs for hotels, bars, homes, pools and restaurants. One material, perfected. One promise, kept: a roof done right, the first time.
