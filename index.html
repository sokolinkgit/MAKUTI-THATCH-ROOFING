Why Makuti Thatch Roofing

We Don't Just Build Roofs. We Weave Legacies.

Every makuti panel is hand-selected, hand-woven and hand-finished by fundis who have perfected this coastal art over decades.

  • Premium Kilifi palm makuti — dense, even, long-lasting weave.
  • Countrywide installation — Nairobi, Coast, Rift, Nyanza, everywhere.
  • Fair, transparent quotations — sent fast, straight to your WhatsApp.
  • Aftercare & maintenance — we keep your roof beautiful for years.
